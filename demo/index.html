Room climate · Live demo

Comfort Compass Card + Mollier hx Card

Two Home Assistant dashboard cards for one question: how comfortable was this room, and why? The compass shows how often the days were in range or too warm, too humid, too dry or too cold. The h,x diagram shows where each day was, with the outside air. This page runs the real cards on simulated sensor data: pick a room, change the comfort zone and the live values, and copy the YAML.
